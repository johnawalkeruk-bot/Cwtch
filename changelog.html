v0.1.12

Direct tools, gentler ground and subtitle fixes

2026-09-25

Changed

  • Select Hoe, Seeds, Watering Can and Shovel with 1–4, or D-pad Up, Right, Down and Left. The radial tool menu is retired.
  • Cycle shovel modes with X on keyboard or X / Square on controller. Put tools away with T or B / Circle; B still goes back in menus.
  • Natural garden rises are capped at 18 cm. Older saved raised patches are softened once; dug-out areas retain their depth.
  • Thump sets every corner and edge of the selected micro-tile to height zero, with a transition into neighbouring ground.

Fixed

  • Arthur’s speech bubble stays hidden until he speaks, including after entering the garden or returning from the village.
  • Putting a tool away cancels its current stroke, particles and sound. Ctrl+T still controls the TARDIS.

Website

  • Added this development changelog and linked it from the game page. Future completed changes will receive notes before manual publication.

v0.1.8

Shape the earth

2026-09-24

Added

  • The shovel has four animated modes with sound: Dig, Pick, Pour and Thump.
  • Dig deforms the ground into a sloping, water-filled hollow. Pick makes a small seed hole; Pour restores dirt; Thump levels the ground.
  • Hold left-click or right trigger to keep using tools while gliding. Each tool uses its supplied sound effects.
  • Ctrl+T and right-stick click toggle the TARDIS landing and takeoff.

Improved

  • Terrain collision, water depth and grass roots follow edits. Deformation and seed holes survive saving and loading.
  • Release packaging now exports to a fresh staging file and excludes temporary packs.

v0.1.0

The foundations of the valley

2026-09-22

Garden and tools

  • Started with exportable 2 m ground chunks, each divided into nine micro-tiles, and expanded the garden to 24 × 24 metres.
  • Connected dirt, hard dirt, grass, long grass, water, deep water, path and stone materials; height-map parallax adds surface detail.
  • A low-floating spirit replaces the visible player, with mouse camera aiming, object selection and a resizing swirling cursor.
  • Added Hoe, Seed Packet and Watering Can models with animated use, seed and water particles, gardening interactions and saved progress.

People and wildlife

  • Imported Arthur and Meera with animation cycles, plus Angus and a valley visitor. Turning is smooth and NPCs avoid one another.
  • Added chicken, hedgehog, badger and dragon models, including procedural movement for rigged animals.
  • Weather-responsive shivering and talking animation/audio behaviour were explored with the visitor models.

Sky and sound

  • Twenty-minute days and twenty-minute nights, a moving sun, clock, stars and a changing sky.
  • Fair, cloudy, light rain, rain, heavy rain, thunderstorm and clearing weather, with wet ground and rain puddles.
  • Birds, wind, stream and storm ambience replaced the music. Layered Welsh mountains, woodland, mist, heather, ferns and gorse frame the garden.
  • Prototype music, the perimeter wall and fixed pond were retired as the open garden took shape.

Menus, guide and village

  • A 16:9 presentation and animated 3D mountain main menu, with birds, day/night weather, the golden CWTCH title and “your slice of the valley.” tagline.
  • Main-menu entry, new garden, options and quit controls; pause-menu Save & Quit.
  • An old leather Field Guide opens from its cover with animated pages, handwritten styling, 3D previews and descriptions for people, animals and plants.
  • Controller movement, camera, menu navigation, shoulder-button categories and directional entry selection.
  • A gravel village street leads to animal, plant, decoration and building shops, with cottage exteriors, interiors, purchases and delivery to the garden.

Development and installation

  • The desktop CWTCH folder became the canonical development project, with source assets, full source documentation and reproducible builds.
  • Windows launcher installation and updates use versioned packages and download checksums; saves are kept separately.
