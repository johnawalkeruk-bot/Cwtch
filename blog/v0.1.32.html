NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

Little sounds, with room for the birds

By Waldas Game Studios · v0.1.32 · 2026-10-03

The buttons have found their voices. Fortunately, they have agreed to use their indoor voices. A purchase gets a little acknowledgement, a menu has a gentle rustle, and the birds do not have to compete with a fanfare every time someone considers a watering can.

Why we changed it

We reviewed all twelve UI SFX styles and chose Organic for its wood-and-water character. It suits the garden, while a style selector leaves the other eleven available for anyone whose chicken prefers science fiction.

Sounds mark actual events: a paid purchase, a confirmed move, a successful sign-in or an upload that really finished. Hovering and routine background work stay quiet.

Mute, interface volume and optional typing sounds are saved separately from the master volume. Typing is off by default, password fields stay silent, and existing visual feedback still carries every message.

What changed

Quiet interface feedback

  • Added native UI SFX feedback for menus, tools, field-guide navigation, confirmed purchases, invalid placement, resident movement and visible wildlife notices.
  • Organic is the quiet default; all 12 sound styles are available in Options, with a separate interface volume, mute switch and optional typing sounds.
  • Login and manual cloud-upload feedback follows the actual result. Background refreshes, routine autosaves, hover and password typing remain silent.
  • Repeated notifications and volume adjustments are limited, typing is not rate-limited, and mute stops interface sounds immediately. Existing music, ambience and visible feedback remain in place.

The slightly muddy bits

These are native Godot audio files, not a browser audio dependency. No repeating UI loops were added over the existing loading music or valley ambience.

Automated checks cover mute persistence, input gating, duplicate suppression, account outcomes and purchase/movement cues. Physical controller listening balance remains a hands-on check.

Audio assets are from UI SFX 0.4.0 under CC0, with source and licence information included.
