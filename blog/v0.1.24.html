NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A soundtrack for the valley, and a save that travels

By Waldas Game Studios · v0.1.24 · 2026-10-02

The cloud save was wearing a decimal point where its identity badge should have been. That is now corrected. Meanwhile, the valley has acquired a soundtrack: morning optimism, afternoon wandering and twilight politely asking everyone to lower their voices.

Why we changed it

The server accepted save format 1, but reading a local JSON file turned it into 1.0 on the next upload. Both describe the same version, yet the existing server compared their text. The client now sends the expected integer without changing anyone’s garden.

The new tracks are selected using the garden clock. Morning in the Vale also accompanies the arrival walk; the afternoon tracks share a shuffled pool with Beautiful Day, and Twilight Pastoral keeps evening and night gentle.

A shared soundtrack follows the garden into the village. It fades down for Arthur, pauses during loading, and avoids playing the same track twice in succession when another suitable one is available.

The v0.1.23 post no longer claims there was nothing to report while a shower of daffodils was clearly happening. The v0.1.19 follow-up now acknowledges that its scenery changes shipped in v0.1.20.

What changed

Cloud saves and loading

  • Fixed local saves being uploaded with a decimal-format version number that the existing cloud server rejected.
  • Kept the clock from drawing before the day/night cycle is ready during garden loading.
  • The loading reveal now uses a prepared camera, avoiding a brief incorrect split-screen view before the arrival walk.

Music through the day

  • Morning in the Vale accompanies the first walk into the garden.
  • Morning and afternoon each have a pool of suitable tracks; shuffle bags play every eligible track before refilling and avoid an immediate repeat when possible.
  • Twilight Pastoral accompanies evening and night, with a lower night-time volume.
  • Music stays soft, fades between time periods, ducks during Arthur’s dialogue and pauses on the main menu or while loading. Garden and village share one soundtrack.

Studio journal

  • Replaced the v0.1.23 placeholder post with its loading-screen story and corrected v0.1.19’s outdated unreleased follow-up wording.

The slightly muddy bits

The upload regression was reproduced locally and its outgoing request format is now tested. A successful retry on the player’s authenticated live account has not yet been verified.

Music scheduling, shuffling and dialogue ducking are tested in code. Twilight Pastoral currently serves both evening and night because no separate night track was supplied.
