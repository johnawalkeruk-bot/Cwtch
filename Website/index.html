Release notes & direct game download ↗

Make yourself at home.

  1. Download & unpack

    Download the launcher ZIP and extract the whole folder.

  2. Open the launcher

    Run CWTCHLauncher.exe from the extracted folder. Keep its accompanying files together.

  3. Let the valley arrive

    The launcher downloads the game. When it is ready, choose Play. Your saved garden stays with you through updates.

An internet connection is needed for installation and updates. The screenshots show development footage; the latest public build may look a little different.
