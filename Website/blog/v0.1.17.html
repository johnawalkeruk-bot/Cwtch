NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A little breathing room

By Waldas Gamer Studios · v0.1.17 · 2026-09-29

The menus had dressed for a flower show. Lovely ambition; rather a lot of elbows at the tea table. We have asked the decorative borders to take the afternoon off and given the valley a little breathing room.

Why we changed it

The decorative frames made the pause and shop menus feel busier than intended. Following player feedback, those panels return to their simpler woodland-green appearance. The daffodil tool wheel keeps its petals because they are the actual selections, rather than extra decoration.

Button hints now sit directly over the scene. A dark text outline helps them stay readable against bright skies and pale ground, without putting another box between you and the garden.

The changelog tells you what changed. This blog adds the why, the rough edges, and screenshots where we have them. Each manual release now preserves its own studio note, so the story will not quietly turn into next week’s story.

What changed

Menus and button hints

  • Restored the clean woodland-green garden pause and village shop menus, removing the decorative petal borders.
  • Button hints now float beside the analogue clock, with outlined text for contrast against the scenery. The daffodil tool wheel and its controller controls stay in place.

Notes from the studio

  • Added a website blog by Waldas Gamer Studios, with release stories, reasons for changes, recorded issues and development screenshots.
  • Manual publishing now creates one blog post per new release and includes it in the website update.

The slightly muddy bits

Controller icons follow the device name reported by the driver. A PlayStation controller presented as a virtual Xbox pad will still show Xbox symbols. Hardware-specific behaviour needs checking on real controllers; the automated checks simulate input.

The new menu treatment follows feedback on the last update. Sometimes the most productive gardening tool is an eraser. No saved-garden format changes are needed for this update.
