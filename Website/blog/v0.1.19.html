NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

Taking the scenic route

By Waldas Gamer Studios · v0.1.19 · 2026-09-29

Updated 2026-10-02 · See the dated development follow-up below.

The scenic route now has a little more to say. Here is the original arrival story, the robin animation work that followed, and a development follow-up from 2 October. The latest scenery changes are still waiting for their next release.

Why we changed it

A new garden should feel like somewhere you have travelled to. The gravel approach connects the northern mountain scenery to the plot, while a short walk gives the valley a chance to introduce itself before Arthur does.

The camera follows the actual hillside surface, with a small walking bob and eased turns. Four staged moments give the journey a little life: something crosses your path, a bird passes, a birch catches your eye, and two animals stand further away.

Early captures showed grass growing through the new gravel. Admirable botanical enthusiasm, but rather poor path maintenance. We cleared the route and moved nearby tree placements back so the approach reads more clearly.

The robin follow-up gave the previously unrigged bird an 11-bone Blender rig with idle, hopping and flight clips, inspired by the supplied motion videos. Its wings can now do the work instead of relying on a whole-model bounce. No tiny pilot licence was required.

Development follow-up, 2 October 2026: the real rabbit, animated robin and bulls replace the arrival stand-ins; varied corner rocks define the plot, and Arthur waits at the entrance. These latest scene changes are not yet released.

What changed

A walk into the valley

  • Added a gravel pathway following the northern mountains and hills down to the garden edge, using the supplied gravel textures.
  • New gardens now begin with a short downhill camera walk, gentle footstep bobbing and smooth looks toward a crossing creature, passing bird, birch tree and two distant animals.
  • The arrival leads into Arthur’s welcome. Escape, B / Circle or Start skips the walk; existing gardens receive the path without replaying the arrival.
  • Time, weather and ambience continue throughout the shared arrival, with normal single-player or split-screen play restored afterwards. The scenery placeholders do not count as wildlife visits.

Previous follow-up: the robin learns its moves

  • Created an editable Blender rig and reference-inspired idle, hopping and flight animations, preserving the source FBX and texture.
  • Connected the clips to garden movement and short low flights, while retaining the 1% water visit requirement and existing saved discoveries.

In development — 2 October 2026 (not yet released)

  • Replaced the crossing placeholder with the supplied rabbit, the flying placeholder with the animated robin, and the distant animals with the supplied bull model.
  • Added a textured rock outside each garden corner, with varied rotations that stay consistent when reloading.
  • Arthur now waits beside the northern entrance for your first arrival and returns to wandering after his welcome.

The slightly muddy bits

The original arrival used deliberately simple stand-ins. The development follow-up replaces their appearances, while keeping them separate from collectible or resident wildlife.

The first robin skin-weight pass pulled some head vertices with a wing. Those weights were corrected before export. The motion is authored from reference, not automatically captured from the videos.

The rabbit and bull files have rigs but no clips: the crossing rabbit currently hops as a whole model and the bulls use gentle breathing motion. Full skeletal gait work is still pending.

The upper mountain route remains scenery. Arrival completion, skipping, Arthur’s waiting state, corner placement and split-screen handover have automated coverage; physical controller testing remains outstanding.
