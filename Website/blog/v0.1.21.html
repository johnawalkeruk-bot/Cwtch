NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A home for your garden, wherever you play

By Waldas Gamer Studios · v0.1.21 · 2026-10-02

The rabbit has stopped attempting a moonwalk, and your spirit now arrives without immediately brandishing a hoe. Meanwhile, the valley is getting cloud saves. These clouds have considerably better filing cabinets than the ones Arthur complains about.

Why we changed it

Starting with empty hands gives the first arrival room to breathe. The rabbit correction makes its little crossing read as an actual journey rather than a disagreement with physics.

The new account screen and private Valley Club dashboard let one garden follow its owner across devices. Coins, wildlife and progress belong together; nobody should need a spreadsheet to remember which hedgehog moved in.

Choosing a local or cloud garden is deliberate, and older uploads cannot silently overwrite newer ones. A backup stays on the computer before a cloud download replaces its local save.

What changed

Garden fixes

  • Both players begin with no tool equipped.
  • Turned the arrival rabbit to face its direction of travel.

Accounts and cloud-save integration

  • Added an Account & Cloud screen with email registration, sign-in, password-reset requests and sign-out.
  • Added explicit local/cloud garden selection, automatic syncing after local saves, revision-conflict protection and local backups before cloud downloads. Offline play remains available.
  • Added a private Your Valley website dashboard showing uploaded coins, animal population, XP, land coverage and wildlife visit/residency dates.
  • Supabase database setup is installed and signed-out access is denied. Redirect URLs are configured; public account launch still needs email delivery, live authenticated acceptance checks and manual publication.

The slightly muddy bits

The development integration is ready for account testing, but the website and game have not been published. Redirect URLs are configured; a working confirmation and recovery email sender is still required before public registration.

Live checks confirmed that signed-out access is denied. Game save/restore and mocked account/dashboard tests passed; two-account authenticated database and real email delivery tests remain pending.

Sign-in is session-only for now. The dashboard shows the last uploaded snapshot, and cloud conflicts pause syncing until the player chooses how to resolve them.
