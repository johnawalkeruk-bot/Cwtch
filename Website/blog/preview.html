NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A louder pause and a left-turning rabbit

By Waldas Game Studios · Development preview · not released

The pause music has found its voice, and the rabbit has received a very specific direction: left. Not a grand expedition, just ninety degrees.

Why we changed it

Pause and Look was too quiet, so its level is now 6 dB higher in both garden and village pauses.

The arrival rabbit needed its model facing turned another quarter-turn to the left.

What changed

Small valley adjustments

  • Raised Pause and Look by 6 dB so the pause-menu music is easier to hear.
  • Rotated the arrival rabbit 90 degrees to the left.

The slightly muddy bits

These are volume and model-orientation adjustments; existing saves remain compatible.

This preview may change before publication.
