NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A purpose for every pair of muddy boots

By Waldas Game Studios · Development preview · not released

Angus has acquired a counter, a set of plans and the right to say “I can have a look at that” professionally. Welcome to McDoogal Construction. Elsewhere, the chickens have stopped treating every crossroads as a coin toss. They now have places to be, things to investigate and a perfectly reasonable objection to getting soaked.

Why we changed it

Random turns made the garden feel busy without giving its residents a reason to move. The new local decision system weighs needs, the valley clock, rain and nearby resources. Arthur checks plots, Meera checks plants, and animals look for useful ground rather than choosing another direction from a hat. These are game behaviours running on your computer; they do not require an online AI service.

A garden full of beds, ponds and residents needs polite traffic. Characters search reachable tiles, respect occupied space and keep their smooth turns. When you ask a resident to move somewhere, your instruction takes priority. Nobody gets to abandon your request because a particularly persuasive clump of grass appeared.

The village needed people behind its shop fronts. Angus now handles building commissions, while Arthur lends his model to the other counters until dedicated keepers arrive. The rooms have their own fittings: feed bowls, plant shelves, furnishings, timber samples and building plans. Interior surfaces also stay dry when the valley weather gets ideas.

The earlier menu and launcher work is included in this update too: consistent uppercase menus, the same six sound sliders from either pause menu, and a launcher dressed in the website’s forest-green and gold colours. Finding the speech slider should not require leaving the garden.

What changed

Life in the valley

  • Replaced random garden wandering with local, goal-driven routines that respond to time, weather, hunger, thirst, energy and reachable resources. Arthur watches the north entrance in the morning and checks plots later; Meera attends planted beds and flowers.
  • Garden animals forage, drink from dry banks, rest on species-appropriate schedules and seek cover in heavier rain. Badgers and hedgehogs are nocturnal; fed peacocks favour open display spots and dragons favour basking ground. The approach rabbit and bulls also keep small home ranges and respond to daylight and rain after the arrival sequence.
  • Routes avoid water, planted beds, placed objects, steep tile transitions and other NPC reservations. Smooth steering and physical collisions remain, and player-directed animal moves take priority over automatic routines.
  • Removed the generic human Visitor from the garden and Field Guide. Wild animal visit requirements and dates remain unchanged. Existing saves remain compatible; daily routines and needs are recalculated when the garden loads.

Village workshops

  • Moved Angus from the garden into McDoogal Construction, the renamed building shop. His Field Guide entry now points visitors to his workshop.
  • Every shop has a keeper. The animal shop, nursery and decorator use Arthur as the placeholder model; Angus keeps the building shop. Keepers greet arrivals, discuss the selected stock and return to waiting.
  • Rebuilt all four interiors with counters, displays, timber detailing, warm lighting and windows that follow the valley clock. Animal crates and feed bowls, nursery shelves, decorator furnishings and construction plans give each shop its own purpose.
  • Fixed the hedgehog shop preview and kept indoor furnishings sheltered from the outdoor rain shader.

Menus, sound and launcher

  • Menu buttons and headings now use consistent uppercase text. Sound Options is available from both garden and village pause menus, sharing the six saved volume controls with start-menu Options.
  • Backing out of Sound Options returns to the still-paused menu and restores focus. Changes to Master, Music, UI, Ambience, Sound Effects and Speech stay in sync across the menus.
  • Restyled the launcher to match the website with forest green, parchment and gold, valley artwork and the Waldas Game Studios logo. Version information, update progress and latest patch notes remain available.

The slightly muddy bits

Angus’s supplied model has one performance clip, so his waiting stance uses a small procedural arm adjustment. Meera has walking and turning clips but no idle clip; she holds a neutral pose when stopped. Dedicated keeper and idle animations can improve this further.

Existing saves remain compatible and animal visit requirements are unchanged. Needs and routine decisions currently restart when the garden loads; this update does not add animal feeding costs, offline simulation or new birth/death rules.

Checks cover species schedules, food and water destinations, actual movement, obstacle routes, occupied tiles, player orders, pausing and all four shopkeepers. Placement and pause-menu regressions also passed. The graphical test shutdown still reports two renderer texture cleanup warnings; no script errors occurred in the completed checks.

This is a local development preview. Nothing has been pushed or published; the updated game, launcher, notes and blog go live through the next manual release.

This preview may change before publication.
