NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A place for everything, including the chicken

By Waldas Game Studios · Development preview · not released

Buying a bench used to be an act of faith: hand over the coins and hope the delivery service shared your artistic vision. Now the bench auditions as a slightly ghostly bench first. The chicken has also acquired a very small traffic-control system.

Why we changed it

The garden is yours to arrange. A translucent preview lets you judge position and rotation before spending anything, while occupied ground, water and steep patches explain why a spot is unsuitable. Cancelling leaves the purse untouched.

A resident should be easy to guide without being teleported across the flowerbeds. One colour stays with the waiting animal and the other marks its destination; after confirmation it walks there, then resumes its usual important investigations. Each split-screen spirit has its own interaction.

X / Square opens tools so A / Cross can handle selection. The save emblem now takes a four-second turn even when a local save is quick. A longer cloud upload keeps it visible; a reassuring spin does not turn a failed upload into a successful one.

What changed

Place purchases in your garden

  • Shop purchases now return you to the garden with a translucent preview. Choose clear, dry ground and rotate with LB/RB (L1/R1), or Q/E on keyboard.
  • Confirm with A / Cross or left click. B / Circle or Escape cancels without spending coins; payment happens only after a valid placement is saved.
  • Placed objects keep their rotation, and purchased animals keep their latest garden position in saves. Older saves remain compatible.

Guide your residents

  • Aim at a resident animal and press A / Cross (R on keyboard) to choose its destination. A gold ring stays with the waiting animal while a blue ring selects the destination.
  • Confirm to let the animal walk around blocked ground; cancel to let it wander again. Player two uses yellow and red markers, with separate placement ownership.
  • Xbox X / PlayStation Square now opens the tool wheel. A / Cross remains the selection and confirmation button; shovel modes are available inside the wheel.

Clear saving feedback

  • The save emblem spins for at least four seconds for a save, and remains visible if a cloud upload takes longer. Local and cloud result messages still report the actual outcome.
  • Reviewed all 13 published studio posts, including the corrected v0.1.19 follow-up and v0.1.23 loading-screen story. Added a repeatable local audit for release details, authors, links and images.

The slightly muddy bits

Placement needs clear, dry ground inside the garden and rejects uneven footprints. Animals need a reachable dry destination; visitors must become residents before they can be guided.

An animal pauses at its chosen destination, then returns to wandering. This is a gentle move request, not a permanent stay command.

Controller interactions have automated coverage for both players, including cancellation and ownership. Physical Xbox and PlayStation hardware still needs a hands-on check.

Old saves remain compatible. New purchase records include rotation; purchased animals update their saved position when the garden saves.

This preview may change before publication.
