NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A proper welcome, and a little sign of saving

By Waldas Game Studios · Development preview · not released

The valley now remembers your name and says hello before handing you a hoe. There is also a small rotating mountain-and-floppy-disk badge, because even a peaceful garden occasionally needs to say: one moment, I am writing this down.

Why we changed it

The welcome explains saving before you start playing, using your username and the supplied save artwork. Controller players get the matching A or Cross prompt.

The save icon stays visible through cloud uploads, while the toast tells you whether the garden reached the cloud or was saved locally.

Sign-in and registration are now separate inside the game as well as on the website. Existing gardeners need not wade through new-account fields.

What changed

Welcome back

  • A personalised welcome popup greets you by username after sign-in, including remembered sessions.
  • Press A / Cross, Enter or Head Outside to continue to the garden, or stay at the main menu.
  • The popup explains autosave and shows the supplied save icon; it also flags any unresolved cloud-garden choice.

Saving and account access

  • The new save emblem rotates slowly during local autosaves and cloud uploads. Local and cloud results remain separate toast messages.
  • In-game Sign In and Create Account now use separate forms. Existing players need only their email and password to sign in.
  • Verified protected login restoration, sign-out cleanup, five-minute autosaves and garden/village account badges.

The slightly muddy bits

Cloud conflicts still need a garden choice in Account & Cloud. The welcome makes that clear rather than silently replacing either save.

Windows protects remembered refresh sessions; revoked sessions still require signing in again. Passwords are not saved.

Tests use disposable player profiles and fixture accounts. Screenshot usernames are examples, not real player data.

This preview may change before publication.
