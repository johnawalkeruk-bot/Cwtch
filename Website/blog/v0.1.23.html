NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

A few petals while the valley wakes

By Waldas Game Studios · v0.1.23 · 2026-10-02

Updated 2026-10-02 · Replaced placeholder copy with the actual v0.1.23 release contents.

The valley now has something to do while it finds its boots. Six blue petals light up in the dark, I Will Wait keeps them company, and a small blizzard of daffodils announces that your garden is ready. The song title is a suggestion, not a demand.

Why we changed it

Loading is part of the first impression. The supplied video gave us a clear starting point: a quiet dark background and six petals waking in blue, recreated as a procedural animation that can keep looping for as long as preparation takes.

Music should make waiting pleasant without becoming the reason you are still waiting. Readiness triggers a brief fade and flower scatter, regardless of where the song has reached.

The first-arrival walk and welcome dialogue begin after the reveal, so their opening moments are not hidden behind a loading screen.

What changed

Loading the garden

  • Added a dark six-petal loading animation inspired by the supplied video, with short messages describing garden preparation.
  • I Will Wait plays while the garden loads and fades out as soon as it is ready; the game does not wait for the track to finish.
  • Copies of the supplied Border-1 daffodil artwork scatter across the screen to reveal the garden.
  • Moved scene-resource loading into a background request and added preparation checkpoints before the arrival walk or Arthur’s welcome begins.

The slightly muddy bits

The animation is inspired by the video rather than playing the video file itself. Each individual preparation step still runs on the game thread and can briefly hold a frame.

A clock redraw could run before its day/night cycle existed during staged setup. A guard for this is included in the following development update.
