NOTES FROM THE STUDIO

Good neighbours, little portraits and a proper pause

By Waldas Game Studios · v0.1.25 · 2026-10-02

The valley has acquired neighbours. Naturally, we gave everyone a tiny mountain portrait and the ability to discover whose garden contains the most suspiciously deliberate hard dirt.

Why we changed it

Unique usernames make gardeners easier to find than email addresses. Automatic portraits give each account an identity without another upload form.

Mutual requests keep comparisons between willing neighbours. A notebook collects the numbers without turning the front page into an agricultural tax return.

Pause and Look gives a pause its own soundtrack. The hoe can wait.

What changed

Your Valley Club identity

  • New accounts choose a unique username; existing players can claim one while keeping their garden.
  • Every profile receives a generated valley portrait, stored with the account and shown on the website.

Neighbours and garden notebooks

  • Search usernames, send and accept friend requests, decline or cancel invitations, and remove friends.
  • Compare accepted friends’ latest uploaded gardens across money, experience, wildlife, gardening, land coverage and more. Full saves remain private.
  • Open your own garden notebook for all recorded statistics.

A quieter pause

  • Pause and Look plays softly when the garden or village is paused; regular music resumes when play continues.

Service setup

  • Requires the new Valley Club Supabase migration and the matching game and website. Existing saves remain compatible.

The slightly muddy bits

The Supabase migration has been applied. Live checks confirm username validation works and signed-out callers cannot read profiles, friendships or the directory. Authenticated signup and friendship flows were tested locally with isolated data; no live accounts were created.

Comparisons reflect uploaded saves and are not cheat-proof rankings. Missing measurements appear as unavailable.

Usernames are permanent in this version. Existing players need to claim one; old game clients must update before registering.
