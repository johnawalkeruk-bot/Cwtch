v0.1.24 · 2026-10-02

A soundtrack for the valley, and a save that travels

By Waldas Game Studios

The cloud save was wearing a decimal point where its identity badge should have been. That is now corrected. Meanwhile, the valley has acquired a soundtrack: morning optimism, afternoon wandering and twilight politely asking everyone to lower their voices.

v0.1.23 · 2026-10-02

A few petals while the valley wakes

By Waldas Game Studios

The valley now has something to do while it finds its boots. Six blue petals light up in the dark, I Will Wait keeps them company, and a small blizzard of daffodils announces that your garden is ready. The song title is a suggestion, not a demand.

v0.1.22 · 2026-10-02

One valley, one studio, fewer keyboards

By Waldas Game Studios

The website and the Valley Club have finally agreed on the curtains. Forest green, parchment and a little gold won the vote. Waldas Game Studios also has its correct name on the door, and a controller can now write an email address without demanding a second piece of furniture.

v0.1.21 · 2026-10-02

A home for your garden, wherever you play

By Waldas Game Studios

The rabbit has stopped attempting a moonwalk, and your spirit now arrives without immediately brandishing a hoe. Meanwhile, the valley is getting cloud saves. These clouds have considerably better filing cabinets than the ones Arthur complains about.

v0.1.20 · 2026-10-02

Familiar faces at the garden gate

By Waldas Game Studios

The temporary rabbit has handed in its notice. A proper rabbit has taken the role, the robin has brought its newly animated wings, and the bulls have agreed to stand in the distance looking as though they own the place. Arthur, meanwhile, has finally checked the arrival time.

The first walk into the valley gave a new garden a proper arrival: gravel underfoot, a little camera bob and several excellent excuses to look around. This article also records the later robin animation and scenery follow-up, which was released in v0.1.20.

Previously, arriving in the garden involved the traditional Welsh practice of appearing out of thin air. We have now installed a path. The rabbit-shaped object using it has declined to pay council tax.
